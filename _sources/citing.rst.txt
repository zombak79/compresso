Citing Compresso
================

If you use Compresso in academic work, please consider citing the framework
and the papers corresponding to the methods used in your research.

Compresso
---------

For the Compresso sparse-representation framework, cite the project:

.. code-block:: bibtex

   @misc{compresso,
     title  = {Compresso: A PyTorch Framework for Sparse Representation Learning},
     author = {Van{\v{c}}ura, Vojt{\v{e}}ch and Giacomo Medda and Spi{\v{s}}{\'a}k, Martin and Ladislav Pe{\v{s}}ka},
     year   = {2026},
     url    = {https://github.com/zombak79/compresso}
   }

method described in:
